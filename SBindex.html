About Us

Surendra Brothers has been manufacturing and trading tarpaulins, industrial rope and covering material out of Ahmedabad since 1980. What began as a small family trading business has grown into an ISO 9001:2015-certified manufacturing and export operation, though it has stayed small enough that the proprietor is personally involved in every order that goes out.

We manufacture, trade and export HDPE and PVC tarpaulins, laminated rolls, industrial rope, LDPE sheet, packing material and biodegradable bags, agricultural mulching film and shade net, and outdoor tents, including genuine Garware tarpaulins. Sizes, thickness and packaging are built to the order rather than picked off a fixed list.
