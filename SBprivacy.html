Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Surendra Brothers ("we", "us") operates this website to share information about our tarpaulin, industrial rope and covering-material business. This page explains, plainly, what happens with your information when you visit.

What this site does not do

This site has no login, no account system, and no order or checkout form. We don't ask you to submit any personal information through it, and we don't run our own tracking or advertising scripts.

What does collect information

  • Google Fonts — the typefaces on this page are loaded from Google's servers, which receive your IP address as part of that request. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Maps embed — the map on our Contact section is provided by Google and is subject to the same Google Privacy Policy.

Contacting us

If you call, WhatsApp, or email us using the links on this site, that conversation happens directly on WhatsApp, your phone network, or your email provider — we only see what you choose to send us, the same as any normal business enquiry, and we use it only to respond to you.

Cookies

We don't set our own cookies. If that changes — for instance, if we add privacy-friendly analytics in future — this page will be updated first.
